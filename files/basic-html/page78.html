                             “PSA Kickoff: The Geniuses Program – Season One”




                   The Geniuses Program at the Faculty of Pharmacy, Mansoura National University,
                   was launched under the patronage of Prof. Cherif Khater, University President, and
                   led  by  Assoc.  Prof.  Ahmed  El-Sheakh,  Director  of  Pharm  D  program  (Clinical
                   Pharmacy), with the supervision of Dr. Bassant Maher, Coordinator of the Pharmacy
                   scientific Association (PSA). As part of the university’s continuous efforts to enrich
                   student life, the program was designed to be a pioneering scientific competition that
                   promotes academic excellence, teamwork, and innovation.

                          The Geniuses program was launched as an educational platform for students to
                   test their knowledge in diverse fields while building critical thinking, teamwork, and
                   creativity.  Its  first  season  featured  four  competitive  episodes  that  highlighted  both
                   students’ skills and the strong organizational efforts of the supporting committees.


                   Program Objectives


                   The Geniuses Program was guided by several clear objectives, which remained central
                   throughout the season:
                   •   Enhancing general knowledge across diverse fields including science, history, arts,
                       literature, and geography.
                   •   Promoting critical and creative thinking by encouraging students to solve problems
                       in new and innovative ways.
                   •   Fostering positive competition in a healthy and motivating academic atmosphere.
                   •   Strengthening  teamwork  and  cooperation  among  participants  as  they  worked

                       together under pressure.
                   •   Developing  quick  response  and  decision-making  abilities,  essential  for  both
                       academic and professional success.
                   •   Creating  an  enjoyable  educational  environment  that  blended  learning  with
                       excitement and creativity.
                   •


                   Episode One – A Strong Beginning


                   The season opened with an energetic first episode, held in Hall 116 at the Faculty of
                   Pharmacy.  Two  enthusiastic  teams,  El-Mazareta  and  +90,  competed  in  a  series  of
                   rounds covering general knowledge, science, chemistry, biology, and problem-solving
                   challenges  under  time  pressure.  The  Media,  Organization,  Documentation,  and
                   Scientific  Research  Committees  at  PSA  collaborated  seamlessly  to  ensure  smooth
                   preparation, from hall arrangements and photography to question development.
